What we look for

Typical requirements.

Every role is a little different, but for driving positions we generally look for:

  • Valid Class 1 licence (or provincial / U.S. Class A equivalent)
  • Clean driver’s abstract and criminal record check
  • Valid passport or FAST card for cross-border runs
  • Verifiable tractor-trailer experience (preferred)
  • Working knowledge of ELDs, hours-of-service and pre-trip inspections
  • A professional attitude with shippers, receivers and border officers
