FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Call dispatch at (506) 269-6967

How quickly can I get a quote?

We aim to reply the same business day. Send the origin, destination, commodity, weight, pallet count and dates through the quote form or call us directly.

Which areas do you serve?

We’re based in Moncton, New Brunswick and run throughout Atlantic Canada, Québec and Ontario, and across the border into the U.S. Northeast, Midwest and Southeast. If you have a lane in mind, ask — we’ll tell you honestly if it’s a fit.

Do you handle customs for cross-border loads?

We file the ACI or ACE eManifest and work directly with your customs broker on PARS/PAPS and release. Customs clearance itself is handled by your licensed broker; we make sure the truck and paperwork arrive ready.

How do I track my shipment?

Our equipment is GPS-tracked and your dispatcher sends status updates at pickup, border crossing (if any) and delivery — plus any time something changes. You can also call or email dispatch at any hour.

Is my freight insured?

Yes. We carry cargo and liability insurance and can send a certificate of insurance before your first load. For high-value goods, let us know in advance so we can confirm coverage.

What do you need from me to book a load?

Pickup and delivery addresses, dates and appointment times, commodity, weight, dimensions or pallet count, any special handling (temperature, liftgate), and contact names at both ends.
