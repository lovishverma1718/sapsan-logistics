Our story

Rooted in Atlantic Canada.
Driven by Excellence.

Founded in Moncton, New Brunswick, SAPSAN LOGISTICS INC. is built on a simple commitment: dependable transportation, honest communication, and exceptional service.

We believe logistics is about more than moving freight — it’s about building trust, respecting commitments, and keeping businesses moving.

From Atlantic Canada to destinations across Canada and the United States, we handle every shipment with professionalism, care, and dedication.

Every shipment matters. Every commitment counts. Every mile builds trust.

Our tagline isn’t a slogan we picked from a list. It’s four commitments we hold ourselves to on every load.

01

Trust

Clear quotes, honest ETAs and no hidden charges. If something goes wrong, you hear it from us first — with a plan attached.

02

Safety

Pre-trip inspections, proper securement, electronic logging and strict hours-of-service. No load is worth a shortcut.

Precision

Drivers and dispatchers who are respected do better work. We invest in ours — and our customers feel the difference.
