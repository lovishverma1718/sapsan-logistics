Built on trust.
Named for speed.

We’re a Moncton trucking and logistics company with a simple idea: if we say a load will be there, it will be. Everything else — the trucks, the tech, the people — exists to keep that promise.

Atlantic Canada
Our story

Rooted in the East. Running the continent.

Sapsan Logistics Inc was founded in Moncton, New Brunswick, with one goal: to be the carrier we would want to hire ourselves. Every shipper has waited on a truck that was “ten minutes out” for three hours. We built this company so our customers never do.

That means honest rates, real-time answers and drivers who take pride in the load behind them. Whether it’s seafood leaving the Bay of Fundy or building materials bound for a job site in Ontario or New England, we treat every shipment as if our name is on it. Because it is.

The name

Why a falcon?

“Sapsan” (Сапсан) is the Russian word for the peregrine falcon — a bird found on every continent except Antarctica, including the cliffs of the Bay of Fundy.

390 KM/H

Headquartered in
the Hub City.

Moncton earned its nickname the honest way: it sits at the crossroads of New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island, on the Trans-Canada corridor west to Québec and Ontario and a short run to the Maine border. It’s the right place to run a carrier from.

All-season operations
How we operate

Built for Canadian winters.

Freight doesn’t stop for weather, and neither does planning. Our team routes around storms, keeps an eye on road reports and talks to drivers before conditions turn — so your customers aren’t the ones who find out about a delay.

  • Electronic logging devices (ELD) and hours-of-service compliance
  • GPS-tracked equipment with proactive status updates
  • ACI / ACE eManifest filing for every cross-border load
  • Cargo insurance and certificates available on request
  • Documented pre-trip and post-trip inspections
